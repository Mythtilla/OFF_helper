01 — What is OFF

Two halves, one place.

OFF stands for Open Freedom Forum. It's a platform being built so people can gather around things they care about — and step aside into private conversation whenever they want.

It isn't a feed you scroll forever. It isn't a wall of other people's highlights. It's one platform with two clear sides, and you decide which one you're in at any moment.

  • talk openly with other people
  • create and join communities
  • find people who share your interests
  • have private conversations
  • appear anonymously, on your own terms
  • customise how the whole thing looks and feels

The two halves are connected on purpose. Communities are where you find people. Private conversation is where you get to know them.

Open space for everyone. You choose the exits.

02 — Why OFF exists

The internet changed how we talk. Then it changed why.

Use the big platforms for a while and the pattern becomes hard to ignore. Conversation becomes the raw material that keeps a feed alive. Every detail you give up — your number, your email, your location — makes you easier to find, track, and target. Not by a conspiracy. By the way the model grew.

And when being public gets uncomfortable, there's no real exit. DMs lose their protection. Blocking is buried. Leaving costs more than staying.

OFF is an experiment in building a calmer, more privacy-conscious corner of the internet — where talking to people is the point, not the byproduct.

Not a replacement for the internet. Not a fix for everything wrong with it. Just a corner of it, built deliberately, by someone who uses the internet a lot and wanted somewhere different to exist.

Some of OFF works today. Some of it is still being figured out. This site tries to be honest about which is which.

05 — The World of OFF

A place to find people.
A place to know them.

The World is the open side of OFF — one big public space where anyone can talk. As it grows, the World opens into smaller rooms: chats for your country, chats for your language, and group chats you can create and share with anyone.

When you want to actually know someone, you add them as a friend and step into a private conversation — just the two of you.

08 — Privacy

Privacy is architecture, not a slogan.

"People should be able to share what they really think — without being found, tracked, or bullied for it."

What that means in practice

  • choose to appear anonymously
  • collect as little data as practically possible
  • no unnecessary tracking
  • meaningful privacy settings, not decorative ones
  • user control over account, conversations and presence
  • security claims based on implementation — never marketing

The honest version

OFF's goal is a place where you can't be tracked and your conversations stay private. That is the point of the project.

But it isn't fully true yet. Real end-to-end encryption is future work. Until it's built and properly verified, OFF must not be compared to mature encrypted messengers — and it won't be.

Privacy is a design goal. Claims follow implementation.
