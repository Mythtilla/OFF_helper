01 — What is OFF

A place to talk, not a place to be farmed.

OFF stands for Open Freedom Forum. It's a platform being built so that people can gather around things they care about — and also step aside into private conversation when they want to.

It isn't a feed you scroll forever. It isn't a dashboard of metrics about your own life. It's a place with two clear halves, and you decide which one you're in at any moment.

  • talk with people
  • create and join communities
  • meet people with shared interests
  • have private conversations
  • customise how the whole thing looks and feels
  • participate without unnecessary platform clutter

Public community

The World is the open side of OFF. Conversations anyone can find and join. Communities form around interests, ideas and questions — the way good forums always did, but without the noise of a modern social feed.

Private conversation

Direct messages are for the things that aren't for everyone. You control who can reach you, message requests sit between you and strangers, and blocking is a first-class action — not something buried five menus deep.

The two halves are connected on purpose. Communities are where you find people. Private conversation is where you actually get to know them.

02 — Why OFF exists

The internet changed how we talk. Then it changed why.

Many modern platforms increasingly organise online interaction around feeds, recommendations, engagement and attention. Conversation often becomes the raw material that feeds that system.

That isn't a conspiracy. It's just the default that emerged once advertising became the main business model of the social web.

OFF is an attempt to explore another direction.

“OFF is an experiment in building a calmer, more privacy-conscious and community-centred corner of it.”

Not a replacement for the internet. Not a fix for everything wrong with it. A corner of it, built deliberately, by someone who uses the internet a lot and wanted something different to exist.

This is an independent project and an evolving product. Some of it works today. Some of it is still being figured out. This site tries to be honest about which is which.

06 — OFF vs the old internet

Forums → Feeds → Messengers → ?

Each era of online communication solved something and lost something else. OFF is an attempt to take the useful parts and leave the rest behind.

01

Forums

Organised, searchable, community-owned.

  • Real discussion threads
  • Strong community identity
  • Slow, fragmented, hard to reach people privately
02

Social feeds

Fast, vast, algorithmically arranged.

  • Everyone in one place
  • Easy discovery
  • Engagement optimisation, ads, tracking, noise
03

Messaging apps

Private, personal, one-to-one.

  • Genuinely private conversations
  • Fast and reliable
  • Weak at open communities and discovery
04

Community + private communication

OFF's intended model.

  • Open communities for finding people
  • Private conversations for keeping them
  • User control instead of engagement pressure
Take the useful parts of old communities and modern messaging, remove unnecessary noise, and build something original.

09 — Privacy

Privacy is architecture, not a slogan.

Privacy is not a word we want to put on a banner.
It has to exist in the architecture.

What that means in practice

  • collect as little data as practical
  • no unnecessary tracking
  • meaningful privacy settings, not decorative ones
  • user control over account, conversations and preferences
  • transparent about limitations
  • security claims based on implementation, not marketing

Privacy is a design goal — but privacy claims should follow implementation.

Currently implemented

Privacy controls & account control

Settings that let you manage visibility, message requests, blocking and your own account. These exist in the current build.

Still being built

Deeper privacy architecture

Stronger identity handling, improved multi-device security and reduced data retention. In progress — not finished, not claimed.

Long-term research

Cryptographic privacy guarantees

Real end-to-end encryption where it is technically appropriate, plus verifiable identity. This is research and future work, not a current property of OFF.
